Security

Your Data is Fortress-Grade Protected

co penny.ai is built ground-up with privacy and security at its core. Here's exactly how we keep your financial data safe.

End-to-End Encryption

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Your financial data is stored with AES-256 encryption at rest, ensuring unauthorised parties can never access your information.

Data Privacy

We follow a strict data minimisation policy. We only collect what's essential to power your financial insights. Your data is never sold to or shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

  • Your transaction data belongs to you — delete it at any time.
  • Anonymous usage analytics only — no financial data involved.
  • No third-party advertising trackers embedded anywhere.

Authentication & Access Control

We use Firebase Authentication with JWT-based sessions. All authenticated sessions are short-lived and automatically expire to minimise the risk of unauthorised access.

  • Google Sign-In and email/password authentication supported.
  • HTTP-only secure cookies for session management.
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) across all API endpoints.

Infrastructure Security

copenny.ai is hosted on secure cloud infrastructure with automated threat detection and real-time monitoring. We conduct regular security audits and dependency vulnerability scans.

  • Regular automated vulnerability scanning of all dependencies.
  • Rate limiting and brute-force protection on all API routes.
  • CORS policies restricted to verified origins only.

Found a Vulnerability?

We take security reports seriously. If you discover a potential security issue, please contact us immediately and we will investigate promptly.
