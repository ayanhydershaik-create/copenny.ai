Getting Started

How do I upload my transactions?

Navigate to your dashboard and select 'Upload Data'. Export your transaction history from your bank as a CSV file and drag it into the neural dropzone. Our AI will automatically categorize and analyze the data.

Account Security

Can Team RedHack see my actual bank login?

Absolutely not. We use secure authentication protocols (Firebase) and do not store or access your bank credentials. We only process the transaction data you choose to share with the platform.

Billing

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

You will retain access to your premium features until the end of the current billing cycle. After that, your data will remains encrypted in our system for 30 days before being automatically purged if the account is not reactivated.

AI Advisor

How accurate is the spending prediction?

The neural advisor requires at least 3 months of transaction history to reach its peak accuracy (typically >92% categorization accuracy). The more you use it, the smarter it gets about your unique lifestyle habits.

Technical

What banks are currently supported?

We support all major banks that offer CSV or QIF transaction exports. Automated real-time integration via Plaid is currently in beta for Pro users in select regions.

Privacy

How do I delete all my data?

Within the dashboard under 'Privacy Settings', you can find the 'Nuclear Option'. Clicking this will instantly and permanently delete your transaction history and neural spending model from our servers.
