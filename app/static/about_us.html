Our Vision

At RedHack, our vision is to create technology that is simple, intelligent, and accessible. With Co Penny, we aim to make financial awareness easier for everyone by transforming raw transaction data into meaningful insights and actionable recommendations.

What We Built – Co Penny

Co Penny is an AI-powered financial assistant that analyzes user spending patterns and provides clear insights into where money goes each month. Instead of manually reviewing transactions, users receive helpful summaries such as spending breakdowns, financial health scores, and potential savings suggestions.

Our goal is to help users:

  • Understand their monthly spending habits
  • Identify their top spending categories
  • Discover opportunities to save money
  • Improve their financial health with simple recommendations
