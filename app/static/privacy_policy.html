Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2025

Protecting your privacy is our highest priority. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

1. Information We Collect

To provide neural financial analysis, we collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email, and authentication data via Firebase.
  • Financial Data: Transaction history, categories, and merchant names provided via CSV or bank integration.
  • Usage Data: Device metrics and interaction logs to improve the AI advisor.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Train your personal neural spending model.
  • Generate financial health scores and saving alerts.
  • Provide support and troubleshoot integration issues.

3. Data Security & Encryption

We employ "Fortress-Grade" security protocols:

  • AES-256 Encryption: All data at rest is encrypted using the industry standard.
  • TLS 1.3: All data in transit is protected using advanced encryption algorithms.
  • Zero-Access Policy: Team RedHack engineers do not have access to your raw financial transactions without your explicit consent for support.

4. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted partners like **Firebase** for authentication and **Google Cloud** for secure processing. We NEVER sell your data to advertisers or third-party brokers.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request a full export of your data or the global deletion of your personal model and account at any time via the user dashboard.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies strictly for session management and security. No advertising or tracking cookies are utilized on our platform.
